method:
  - title: Build the base
    text: Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook the onion 5 minutes until the edges catch color. Add garlic, cumin and smoked paprika and stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
  - title: Simmer the beans
    text: Add the chipotles, beans, water and salt. Simmer 8 minutes, mashing about a third of the beans against the side of the pan so the whole thing thickens and clings.
  - title: Char the tortillas
    text: Toast the tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry cast iron pan, 20 seconds a side, until blistered. Stack them in a towel to steam soft.
  - title: Assemble
    text: Spoon beans onto each tortilla and top with pickled onion, cotija, cilantro and a hard squeeze of lime.
notes: The mash step is what separates this from beans on a tortilla. For vegan tacos, swap cotija for toasted pepitas and a spoon of cashew crema.
