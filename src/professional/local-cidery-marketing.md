A marketing analytics practicum that combined consumer segmentation, text analysis, and creative strategy to help East Bower Cider Company (EBCC) better understand its audience and optimize its advertising and social media presence.

## The problem

East Bower Cider Company wanted to better understand its target customers and how they interacted with social media. Our team aimed to identify customer groups, uncover key sentiment drivers, and translate those insights into recommendations for more effective advertising and social media messaging.

## The approach

To solve our problem, I conducted a consumer segmentation and text analysis in R to identify customer groups and key sentiment drivers to specific marketing media and platforms. We combined survey analysis with social media research, including an examination of existing customer interactions and content, to understand where EBCC’s audience engages with local businesses and what types of creative resonate with them.

Based off of the analysis conducted, we then designed mock social media creatives and assess their expected engagement impact using historical consumer interaction data. We also developed customer personas and recommendations around user-generated content, event promotion, and behind-the-scenes storytelling.

## The result

Translating data-driven consumer insights into actionable recommendations for EBCC’s advertising and social media strategy contributed to a 38% increase in engagement. Our recommendations focused on improving Instagram content, strengthening customer familiarity with EBCC’s atmosphere, and connecting paid advertising with authentic organic content.

The project also provided valuable experience in client-facing analytics, collaborative problem-solving, and communicating research findings through a clear, accessible presentation.

## What I learned
This practicum taught me that effective data science extends beyond analysis. Balancing a demanding academic schedule, multiple practicum projects, and teaching assistant responsibilities challenged my time management. When team participation declined, I stepped into a leadership role to help keep the project moving and ensure we delivered for our client.

The experience reinforced the importance of clear communication, accountability, and taking initiative when working toward a shared goal.
